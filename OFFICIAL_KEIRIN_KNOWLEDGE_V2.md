# Official KEIRIN knowledge reflected in this build

Reviewed against KEIRIN.JP official guide / glossary / rules on 2026-08-09.

## Line behavior
- A line is cooperation, not permanent welding.
- The second rider supports the self-powered rider.
- The second rider can block a rear attack.
- The second rider can create a gap to make the attacker cover more ground.
- When another line passes, later riders can switch and follow the new line.
- Competition for the bante position exists and may become a separate future interaction model.

## Rule constraints represented as engine principles
- no unlimited cross-cutting
- no unlimited inward pressure
- no unlimited outward forcing
- no unrestricted inside overtaking
- bounded block duration and bounded lateral target

Exact disqualification geometry is not claimed in v2.  That belongs to a later adjudication-calibration layer.
